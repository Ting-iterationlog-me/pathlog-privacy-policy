PathLog Privacy Policy

English

This app does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

All data created and used within the app (such as tasks, notes, or activity records) is stored locally on your device only. The data is not transmitted to any external servers.

This app does not use analytics tools, advertising SDKs, or third-party tracking services.

We do not require user registration or login, and we do not offer cloud synchronization.

As a result, your data remains fully private and under your control at all times.

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact: ting@iterationlog.me
