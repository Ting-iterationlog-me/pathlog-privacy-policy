English

Data Collection
This app does not collect, store, or share any personal information. We do not require user registration or login, and we do not offer cloud synchronization.

Local Storage Only
All data created and used within the app (such as tasks, notes, or activity records) is stored locally on your device only. The data is not transmitted to any external servers.

Third-Party Services
This app does not use analytics tools, advertising SDKs, or third-party tracking services. No data is shared with any third party.

Your Control
Since all data remains on your device, you have complete control over your information. Uninstalling the app will remove all associated data from your device.

Children's Privacy
This app does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13. As the app collects no data at all, no special restrictions apply.

Changes to This Policy
Any changes to this privacy policy will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

Contact Us
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: contact@iterationlog.me
